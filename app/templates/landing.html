QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

FinPilot AI, clearly explained.

What documents can I upload?

FinPilot AI supports PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, and DOCX business documents, including invoices, receipts, bills, and expense statements up to 16 MB.

Will AI make up financial numbers?

No. FinPilot AI is built on verifiable evidence. All answers are derived directly from facts extracted and stored in your own workspace. It preserves provenance and confidence alongside extracted figures.

Can I review anomalies before acting?

Yes. Every anomaly includes a severity rating, clear explanation, evidence provenance, related source documents, and recommended next steps for your team.

Is this financial or lending advice?

No. FinPilot AI is an analytical decision-support SaaS. Professional human judgment remains essential for accounting, lending, tax, legal, or investment decisions.
